# Changelog

## 0.3.0 – first public release

- **Polaroid layout:** a square photo in a classic Polaroid-style frame, as wide as the
  paper, with a dashed line to cut along.
- **Date stamp:** the date the photo was taken (dd/mm/yyyy) in orange, red or yellow LCD
  digits, or orange dot matrix. Very 1995.
- **Zoom:** mouse wheel or slider, limited so the print never drops below the printer's
  resolution.
- **Print selected** next to **Print all**.
- Two photos per sheet now always have a 1 mm cutting line, and with an odd number of photos
  the last one is printed full size instead of next to an awkward empty half.
- New name (Open DyePrint, it used to be called Tintype) and a new icon. Old settings come
  along automatically.

## 0.2.0

- Opens iPhone **HEIC** photos.
- **Adjustments** per photo (exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, warmth)
  and an **Auto** button.
- **Drag the crop** in the preview.
- **Two photos per sheet.**
- Copies per photo, reorderable photo list, Delete key.
- Preview shown in the monitor's colour profile.
- Tells you when the printer is out of paper or ink, or offline.
- Builds as a stand-alone `.exe`, with an optional "Print with ..." entry in Explorer's
  right-click menu.

## 0.1.0

- First working version: one photo per sheet, fill or fit, borderless, your own ICC
  profile and rendering intent, printing 1:1 at the printer's resolution with Windows'
  colour management switched off. The first print matched. Much rejoicing.
